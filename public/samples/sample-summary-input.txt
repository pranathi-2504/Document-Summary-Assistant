Quarterly Operations Update

Revenue increased 14% year-over-year, driven by enterprise contracts and reduced churn in the mid-market segment.
Customer retention improved from 88% to 92% after introducing guided onboarding and quarterly success reviews.
Support ticket response times dropped by 21% after automation of repetitive triage tasks.

Key risks include delayed integration timelines for two strategic customers and increased infrastructure costs (+9%).
The product team plans to prioritize reliability improvements, cost optimization, and usage analytics in Q4.

Recommended next steps:
1) Finalize customer integration playbooks.
2) Publish monthly cost transparency reports.
3) Expand customer training sessions to reduce avoidable support tickets.
